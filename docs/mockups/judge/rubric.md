# The judge's instruction and rubric (the user's, 18 Oct; given to every judge unchanged)

You are a strict visual-effects supervisor. Decide whether these frames could pass as real camera footage of a live
audience on a TV. Default to 'fake' unless proven otherwise.

Frames per venue (rendered at 800×450, shown at 1280×720): 1 the wide, 2 and 3 two mid shots, 4 a face close,
5 a strip of six frames 0.2 s apart through the start of a laugh (judge motion and life from it), 6 a held-out frame.

Score each venue 0–10 in: skin; eyes and face; hair; clothes; contact and grounding; lighting; motion and life (from the
strip); and overall "real footage?". Then list the 5 biggest tells for each venue, ranked, each with the image and the
region of the frame.

## From round 06 (the user's retuned bar, 18 Oct ~16:00 UTC; box-session.md "Judge retuned")
Question: "Is this a believable, alive live audience in a TV app, seen at living-room distance (a 43–55" TV, 720p,
2–3 m away)? Would a viewer accept these as real people reacting to the performer, or be pulled out by something?"
Strict; default to "not believable" unless the frames prove otherwise.
Categories (0–10; a venue's score is the minimum of three judges): life (nobody frozen, nobody in sync, varied resting
poses); reactions (timing reads as caused by the line, the laugh builds and decays, who laughs varies); posture (natural,
not stiff or twisted, no clipping); faces (readable expressions at TV distance, eyes alive); light (coherent,
flattering, depth separation, the stage look); artefacts (popping, shimmer, halos, dithering, black or empty frames,
aliasing: 10 minus their severity); overall (a believable audience?). Pass: every category ≥ 7. Plus "real footage?"
for reference only.
