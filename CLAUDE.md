## Honesty rules (every screen, doc and the video must agree; full text in `docs/concept-encore.md`)
1. **The room reacts to what was measured; the ledger reports what was measured.** Every `Reaction` carries a measured `why`. Code: `packages/crowd/src/engine.ts` (`landingScore`, `reduceSet`).
2. **A tougher venue changes the crowd, never the score.** `landed` counts at `HOUSE_STANDARD` (0.55), the room reacts at `venue.threshold`. Code: `scorecard()` in `engine.ts`, thresholds in `packs.ts`.
3. **"Unsure" is grey and never counts against the performer.** Quiet/short lines are `score: null`; words off makes `wpm`/`fillers` `null`, never 0. Screens print "not measured".
4. **No audio leaves the phone, and the app never sends audio anywhere.** Words leave only when the performer switches them on; the TV shows the mode (`local` / `service` / `off`). Code: `apps/companion/src/set/words.ts`, `Live.tsx`.
5. **The MC is the model or it is silent.** Every MC word (debrief, green-room answer and the intro, between-bit, heckle and closing cards during the set) is written by the model from measured facts. There are no template or rule-based lines. When the model can't answer in time, or the honesty guard rejects its answer, the MC says nothing and the screen states why; the show never waits for it. Code: `services/mc-agent/src/mc_agent/mc.py`, `packages/crowd/src/mc.ts`, `packages/shared-ui/src/encore/useMc.ts`.
6. **Nothing is faked for the demo.** No scripted reactions in the video; dead bits stay in.
7. **Content is licensed.** Photos, clips and sounds have `CREDITS.md` next to them under `packages/shared-ui/src/assets/`; CC BY items are credited on the bill screen. No song catalogue, no brands in the video.
8. **Every set ends on OK or on the clock (≤ 5 min).** Vega closes apps after ~5 minutes without remote input.
9. **No react-native-svg on the TV** (fails on the SDK 0.24 simulator); Views, Images, Animated and video only. The QR code and the pitch trace are Views.

## Architecture

One set, end to end:
1. The TV (couch-link **host**) opens a room; the bill screen shows the QR code. The phone (**player**) joins at `https://<host>:5173/?room=CODE`, starts the microphone and reports `mic {ready, words}`.
2. OK on the bill sends `arm {setId, act, venue, persona, durationSec, startAt}`. The phone waits for `startAt` on the relay clock, then t = 0 on its own audio clock, and reports `started {startedAt}` so the TV aligns.
3. The phone streams events only (and ignores its mic while the TV sends `hush {untilMs}` for a crowd cue or the MC's voice, so the room's own sound never counts as the performer): `voice` (speaking on/off), `line` (a phrase ended: times, levels, pause before, optional words/fillers), `words-for` (late words), `pitch` batches (singing), `finished`.
4. The TV feeds those into the pure engine (`reduceSet`) plus a `tick` ten times a second; the engine decides reactions, cuts (the `shot`), cards, heckles and the scorecard. The screen renders the state; the sound and the light pulse key off new reactions.
5. After the set the TV builds a `SetRecord`, asks the MC service for a `debrief` (or shows the MC as unavailable, with the reason), shows the cut sheet, and keeps the last six records for the green room and the MC's memory.

Layering:
- **`packages/crowd` is the product's brain and is pure.** Types and protocol in `src/types.ts` / `src/messages.ts` (the contract every other part is built against); packs and venues in `packs.ts` (`packsAsJson()` is what sits on S3); the reducer, landing score, director and scorecard in `engine.ts`; the crowd drive for the 3D crowd in `drive.ts`; the cut sheet's ledger rows ("not measured", never a zero), the measured next-room rule and the set counter in `ledger.ts`; the moments the MC speaks at (`SetMoment`, filled by `mc-line` actions). No React, no Date.now, no Math.random; ES2020 and TypeScript 4.8 clean.
- **Content judging (2026-10-12): with words on, the room also judges what you say.** `packages/crowd/src/content.ts` finds deterministic patterns in each line's words (Tier 1: stand-up callback, wordplay, triad, reversal, tag, specific; poetry end/internal rhyme, alliteration, meter, anaphora, volta; speech triad, anaphora, contrast, question, call to action, quotable; singing, from the pitch with words on or off: held note, final note, range), and `landingScore` adds their per-act weights to the timing score, capped at `CONTENT_CAP` (0.3), never subtracting. Tier 2 is one model estimate per line: with `SetConfig.judge` the TV sends `judgeRequests(state)` to the MC service (`kind: judge`, `judge.py`: one JSON-mode call to `MC_JUDGE_MODEL_ID`, default ministral-3-8b, within `MC_JUDGE_TIMEOUT_S` 0.35 s; for stand-up mixed 0.7/0.3 on a percentile scale with the CPU humour model `humor.py` when `MC_HUMOR_MODEL_DIR` is set, files in `~/models/humor-int8` on the build box, `uv sync --group humor`; chosen and measured in `docs/judge-calibration.md`). `useSetSession` sets `judge: true` when the MC is configured and sends each line once through `requestJudge` (`useMc.ts`), then hands back `{type: 'judge', verdict}` or `judge-none`; the engine weighs the score above the act's `JUDGE_FLOOR` (stand-up 0.70 on the percentile scale, poetry and speech 0.5), waits for it at most `JUDGE_DEADLINE_SEC` (0.9 s after the line) and otherwise decides on timing and patterns, saying so in the why. The phone sends the recogniser's interim words with each line (`LineEvent.interim`; the finals follow as `words-for`). Whys name each pattern and label the model's part an estimate; captions lead with the strongest reason ("LAUGH · a callback to your first bit · 0.6 s beat"); `Scorecard.content` (null when words are off, or when a singing set heard no pitch; `longestHoldSec` is null then too, never 0) and the cut sheet's content row count the patterns; reactions and records carry `features` for the MC's debrief. Words off: timing only, as before; a tougher venue still changes only the threshold (both tested).
- **`couch-link` is game-agnostic** (rooms, `send(to, data)`, reconnect with seat resume, clock sync). The wire envelope (`protocol.ts`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION`) is shared with `services/relay`; change both together. The relay's routing (`relay.ts`) is transport-independent; `server.ts` is only the `ws` wrapper.
- **`pitch-judge` runs only on the phone** (singing: `createPitchTracker`; `rms` for the level).
- **The MC service** mirrors the TypeScript contract in pydantic (`models.py`), dispatches `debrief` / `chat` / `line` / `judge` by `kind`, uses a fresh Strands agent per request (the judge makes one direct JSON-mode call on the same warm connection instead), and answers with `{note}`, `{reply}`, `{line}`, `{verdict}` or `{unavailable: {reason}}`; nothing rule-based. `MC_PROVIDER` = `mantle` (default: Bedrock's OpenAI-compatible endpoint `https://bedrock-mantle.<region>.api.aws/v1`, Strands `OpenAIModel` with a SigV4-signing httpx client in `sigv4.py`) or `converse` (Strands `BedrockModel`, for when Claude unlocks). `MC_MODEL_ID` defaults to `zai.glm-5` on mantle (chosen 2026-10-11 over Qwen3 235B, Kimi K2.5, GLM-4.7, GPT-OSS, Mistral Large 3 and DeepSeek V3.2 for clean English and persona voice) and `anthropic.claude-opus-5-5` on converse; `PORT` (default 8080; agents test on 8091 so the user's :8080 stays up); `MC_REGION` (else `AWS_REGION`, else ap-south-1), which should match the region of the box the MC runs on: the judge's 0.35 s budget doesn't survive a cross-region hop (from the us-west-2 emulator box, ap-south-1 took 234 ms just to connect and the judge was in time on 0 of 49 lines, QA 2026-10-12), so on `BOX=android` the MC runs with `MC_REGION=us-west-2` (`~/emu/svc.sh` sets it) and on `BOX=build` with ap-south-1; `MC_TIMEOUT_S` 8 (debrief, chat) and `MC_LINE_TIMEOUT_S` 3.5 (moment lines during a set). `guard.py` never writes words: it rejects output that quotes a number the payload can't produce (in digits or in words; a hedged honest rounding such as "nearly fifteen seconds" for 14.7 passes, and "nearly" / "just over" must point the right way and be the number's nearest rounding: 4.1 is "just over four", never "nearly four and a half"), leaves a placeholder, omits a required "not measured", contradicts the `next` rule (or puts anything above the Arena), names a content pattern the payload never counted ("wordplay carried the room" with no wordplay found; a negated mention or advice in the fix passes), suggests a room move in its words that contradicts `next` ("try the next room up" with 'again'), puts clock times on cards, or puts a clock time, a decimal or an abbreviation in `spoken`; the model gets one retry with every reason at once (and, for the debrief, its checklist again: the house rule's direction, a words-off set's "not measured" sentence and the only patterns it may name, which also leads the first prompt), then the answer is `{unavailable: {reason: rejected}}`. It only trims lengths and strips markdown. The guard checks numbers, not meaning, so prompts must forbid unmeasured claims. The MC's voice is Amazon Polly (`voice.py`, plan in `docs/voice-plan.md`). Answers to `speak: true` carry `audio: {format: 'mp3', url, fallbackUrl?, voice, engine}`, a **presigned Polly SynthesizeSpeech HTTPS URL** (600 s, signed locally in about 1 ms, never logged) for the debrief's `spoken`, the reply's `spoken` or the intro's `text`. The TV streams it itself, so Polly is outside the model's budget; base64 is gone (Android's react-native-video can't play `data:`, and Vega's w3cmedia opens only HTTPS). `spoken` (up to four short sentences) is written for the ear: numbers in words with honest hedges, moments in words ("about twenty seconds in"), no clock times; `facts.py` gives each number's spoken form, and the screen keeps the exact numbers. Every engine reads SSML (`voice.ssml()`: a beat where the model wrote "...", Dev at 85% with a gap after each sentence; generative takes rate only in steps). The voices are Kajal (warm), Brian (sarcastic), Stephen (strict) and Matthew (deadpan), chosen 2026-10-11 and kept after the v2 auditions (2026-10-12, `docs/voice-samples/v2/index.md`: every reachable Polly voice and engine plus Nova 2 Sonic, scored for predicted naturalness; Nova reads faithfully only without a persona style and can't be presigned); `MC_VOICE_<PERSONA>` overrides them, and `MC_VOICE_DEADPAN=Gregory` brings back long-form. The premium engine (generative in `MC_VOICE_PREMIUM_REGION`, default ap-southeast-1; long-form only in us-east-1) is used for the intro, debrief and chat, with the same words on neural (`MC_VOICE_REGION`, default the MC region) as `fallbackUrl`. A pair is signed only if DescribeVoices lists it for that region. `MC_VOICE_SIGNER_ROLE_ARN` signs with an assumed role. The model has one warm connection per region and a 45 s keep-alive (`MC_WARM=0` turns it off). Only the intro, debrief and green-room replies are voiced; the end card and the between-bit and heckle cards are text only. On the TV, `world/McVoice.tsx` runs two preload slots with a 250 ms start buffer, a fallback and start deadlines. The phone is hushed on a 1.5 s lease, renewed while the voice actually sounds. **AgentCore (staged, 2026-10-11):** `MC_BACKEND=agentcore` plus `MC_AGENTCORE_ARN` turns the local service into a keyless forwarder to the runtime (`forward.py`, `deploy/README.md`). ## Conventions
- Shared logic lives in pure, tested TypeScript packages (`packages/*`); the apps stay thin. `packages/*` are consumed as source: ES2020-safe for Hermes (no `Array.prototype.at`, no Node APIs) and TypeScript 4.8-safe (no `satisfies`, no `const` type parameters). RN 0.72's URL polyfill lacks `searchParams.set`: build URLs with strings (`joinUrl`).
- Keep the TypeScript contract (`packages/crowd/src/types.ts`) and the pydantic models (`services/mc-agent/src/mc_agent/models.py`) in step; camelCase on the wire; `null` means not measured.
- Design: follow the direction contract in the surface brief (full-bleed photographic rooms, hard cuts, lower-third with the red rule, no panels/glass/icons, secondary text gold-dim never grey); comps in `docs/mockups/`. UI work goes through the Impeccable skill (craft floor before edits; finish review + DESIGN.md at the end).
- Assets: every photo, clip and sound has a row in the folder's `CREDITS.md` with licence and source; nothing without a licence that allows commercial use and redistribution.
- Prettier: 120 columns, single quotes, trailing commas (`yarn format`). `apps/vega` keeps the sample's own ESLint 8 and Prettier 2 configs.
- Log Amazon/AWS tooling friction in `docs/friction-log.md` **as it happens** (up to a +10% judging bonus). Record product feedback in `docs/product-feedback.md`.
- Commit messages and PR bodies follow the attribution rules in the session's system prompt.
