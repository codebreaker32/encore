with the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatars"), © 2020 Microsoft, under the MIT licence (text below). They are made for
the same Bip01 (3ds Max Biped) rig as the avatars in `../people/`, so no retargeting was needed.

## Edits (all made by `tools/anim/fetch.sh` and `tools/anim/build_anims.mjs`, reproducible)

- Converted FBX → glTF with FBX2glTF 0.9.7 (the same converter as the avatars, so bone frames match).
- Kept 23 bones (Bip01 to the toes; no fingers or face). Resampled at the rate in `tools/anim/clips.txt` (10–24 fps)
  and trimmed long idles, talks and laughs to the length given there. Rotations stored as int16 x, y, z (w ≥ 0 rebuilt
  on load), the Bip01 translation in millimetres.
- Walks: the forward root motion is removed (an in-place cycle) and kept as speed and stride, so the page plays the
  cycle by distance walked. Sitting down and standing up: the root path is re-based so the seated end is at the seat.
